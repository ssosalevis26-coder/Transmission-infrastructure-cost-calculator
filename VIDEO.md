# Demo video guide

1. Open the deployed URL in a fresh browser window. Start a screen recording with
   your microphone enabled. Show the URL and introduce the planner.
2. Open **New estimate**. Enter Texas, Solar + storage, 50 MW, an $80M budget,
   and 2 line miles. Calculate. Point out the $120M capital allowance, $67,600 in
   raw materials, $40,067,600 shortfall, and the two alternatives.
3. Save the hybrid. Return to the form, switch to solar, calculate and save.
   Compare the two rows and explain the budget balance.
4. Open **Transmission**. Explain quantity per mile, unit price, and the
   exclusions for the raw materials allowance.
5. Open **Assumptions**. Explain the four-hour battery and the difference between
   up-front capital allowances and Lazard's historical LCOE benchmarks.
6. Verify the recording has legible text and audible narration. Upload it where
   the TA can play it, verify the sharing link in a signed-out window, and add it
   to the README under Submission materials.
